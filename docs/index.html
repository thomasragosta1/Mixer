Quick answers

Where are my recordings? On your iPhone, inside the app, and in your own iCloud if you turn on Settings → Sync with iCloud. Nothing goes to us. Deleted projects wait in Recently Deleted (the bin icon on the projects list).

How do I record over part of a track? Drag the waveform to the spot, tap the track to arm it, and press record. Only what you record over is replaced.

My overdub is a little early or late. Wired headphones give the tightest timing. Bluetooth headphones add delay.

How do I bring in a Voice Memo? In Voice Memos, tap the recording, then Share → Four-Track. Choose a new project or add it as a track to an existing one. You can also use the import button on the projects list.

How do I delete a track? Press and hold it, then drag it to the bin.

How does iCloud sync work? Turn on Settings → Sync with iCloud on each device signed in to the same Apple Account. Projects you edit appear on the others the next time you open the app there. A project you have open updates after you close it; if the same project was changed on two devices, the most recent edit wins.
